SpectralForge Chimera

A guitar and bass amp simulator. Use it as an AU or VST3 plug-in in your DAW, or as a standalone app.

After installing:

  1. Open SpectralForge Chimera from Applications (or load it in your DAW).
  2. Pick a sound in Easy view.
  3. Press AUTO SET and play normally for about half a minute. Chimera sets your input level, noise gate and tone.

Not sure what to choose? Click Continue on each page; the defaults work for most people.

This is a free beta. After its expiry date, Chimera opened from then on is muted; install an update to keep playing. Projects and presets can still be opened and saved.
