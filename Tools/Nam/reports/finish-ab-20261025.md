## Reading

- **The low-LR finish helps.** Mean full ESR fell from 0.032 to 0.025.
  Fimbulvetr is now at the edge of two limits: full ESR is 0.0103 against <0.01,
  and the active median is 0.0057 against ≤0.005. Its active p95 is still
  3.6 times the limit.
- **Amplitude weighting and the overshoot penalty did not help** at these
  weights. Ragnarok's peak error moved only from +2.21 to +1.99 dB, and the
  other channels were slightly worse.
- **Five-excerpt selection is still unreliable.** For three channels the final
  step beats the chosen checkpoint on the full signal. Nidhoggr kept step 0:
  0.0369 selected against 0.0298 at the final step. The five excerpts cover
  about 0.85 s of the 24 s validation signal. Selection should use the full
  validation signal (`--validation-selection full`).
- **The worst active windows are not quiet passages.** The worst 5% of active
  windows sit at a normal reference level, about −20 dBFS, as do the others.
  Only two active windows across all channels fall below −40 dBFS. So the gap
  to the p95 limit is real error at playing level, not an artifact of the
  ESR's relative scale.
