# Náströnd TONE3000 package as A2-Lite + A2-Full, 2026-10-31

The approved package ([release-approval-20261029](release-approval-20261029.md))
held one bare A2-Full WaveNet per channel. The TONE3000 plugin loads such a
file, but the TONE3000 site listed the upload as a custom model, not A2.

## Cause and fix

TONE3000's own A2 training config (`nam/train/_resources/config_model_packed.json`
in the pinned trainer) trains two tiers together, `channels_3` and
`channels_8`, and exports them as one `SlimmableContainer`: A2-Lite below
slimmable size 0.5 and A2-Full up to 1.0. The plugin's **NAM A2 Size**
setting (A2-Lite / A2-Full) picks the tier.

- `train_a2.py --width 3` trains A2-Lite.
- `package_a2.py --lite-run` writes each channel as that container. Lite uses
  Full's fixed output scale, so switching tiers keeps the calibrated level.
  The packager checks Lite's player parity and fidelity too.
- `ChimeraTone3000Tool` accepts the container, using the player's own A2 gate,
  and renders either tier.

The owner uploaded the new package. TONE3000 lists it as **A2**, and switching
the plugin's NAM A2 Size to A2-Lite changes the sound, so both tiers are in
place.

## A2-Lite

The five Lite models were trained from scratch on 7,680 s of a fresh capture.
The capture's held-out audio and renderer are byte-identical to the release,
so Lite is judged on the same signals as Full. Training ran 50,000 steps at an
LR of 0.004 with a 12,000-step half-life. Two channels were then retrained:
Ragnarok with a peak penalty to bring its level in, and Fenrir with the
block-normalized loss for its quiet window. A2-Full is unchanged from the
release.

Lite is **report-only**, by owner decision: release profile v3 applies to
A2-Full, the default tier.

| Channel | Validation full ESR | Test full ESR | Validation level RMS / peak dB | Test level RMS / peak dB | Validation quiet peak dBFS |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---:|
| Fenrir | 0.0581 | 0.0637 | −0.23 / −0.36 | −0.23 / −0.46 | **−58.5** |
| Surtr | 0.0575 | 0.0570 | −0.16 / −0.28 | −0.19 / −0.38 | −70.3 |
| Nidhoggr | 0.0610 | 0.0603 | −0.24 / +0.62 | −0.27 / +0.21 | −67.3 |
| Fimbulvetr | 0.0257 | 0.0283 | −0.16 / +0.63 | −0.19 / −0.04 | −70.1 |
| Ragnarok | 0.0708 | 0.0813 | −0.37 / +0.94 | **−0.57** / +0.34 | −67.8 |

- Lite's error is 3 to 4 times Full's, as expected for about a seventh of the
  parameters.
- Fenrir Lite misses the quiet peak limit on validation by 1.5 dB, after two
  retraining rounds.
- Ragnarok Lite misses the RMS level limit on test by 0.07 dB.
- Every other quiet and level limit passes on both splits.

## Status

The Lite + Full zip (`Nastrond-A2-TONE3000-LiteFull.zip`, SHA-256 in the JSON)
is deployed. Its A2-Full tier is the approved release. No separate listening
acceptance of the Lite tier is on record, beyond the owner's check that it
loads and sounds different.
