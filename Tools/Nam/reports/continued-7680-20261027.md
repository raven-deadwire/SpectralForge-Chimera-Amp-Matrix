## Reading

- **Doubling the data again gave a smaller gain:** mean test full ESR fell 7%.
  The previous doubling gave 14%. Returns from data are diminishing.
- **Fixing the first-step LR removed the immediate jolt, but not the whole
  dip.** At step 500, Surtr moved from 0.0186 to only 0.0207, against
  0.022 → 0.047 in the previous run. But every channel was still worse than the
  start once the LR reached its 0.0005 peak, and stayed so until about step
  14,000 (LR about 0.0002). For a converged model with a fresh optimizer, a
  peak LR near 0.0002 looks sufficient.
- Fimbulvetr passes the full-ESR limit on both splits; its validation active
  median, 0.0035, is within the limit, but on test it is 0.0062. Ragnarok's
  peak error is down to +1.49 dB, still outside ±1 dB. The active p95 stays at
  7–9 times its limit on four channels.
