## Measured causes and remaining hypotheses

1. **Insufficient convergence under the tested budget.** Four legacy channels
   still select the final update; their 500-to-5000 validation curves continue
   downward. The official schedule completes only four virtual epochs and ends
   at LR 0.00390486, versus legacy 0.000707107. Official validation fluctuates,
   including Ragnarok 0.426561 at step 2000 and 0.242057 at step 5000. A lower
   LR is a justified controlled experiment, not proof that LR alone caused
   every error or that longer training will eventually satisfy A2 constraints.
2. **Training/export response mismatch.** Training previously optimized raw
   output while validation and export subtracted the model's zero-history
   response. Legacy Surtr removes 0.232807 raw output units. Official Fimbulvetr
   changes from approximately 0.0518 raw ESR to 0.0546 after that correction.
   The new opt-in objective differentiates through the same correction used
   at export, preserving signal-dependent DC and asymmetric distortion.
3. **Active audio is the main remaining error.** Legacy Ragnarok attributes
   91.2% of its error energy to the two pluck sections and 8.2% to broadband
   excitation; terminal decay contributes only 0.6%. Its peak error is +3.018
   dB. A tail-only fix cannot correct attack shape, level or channel response.
   Fenrir/Surtr/Nidhoggr also show large active-window failures.
   Legacy correlation peaks at zero lag for all five channels within ±32
   samples, which argues against a simple bulk-delay error. Ragnarok's
   250–1000 Hz diagnostic ESR is 0.38334; Surtr and Nidhoggr have 1–4 kHz ESRs
   0.22241 and 0.18572. These filtered diagnostics do not replace the unchanged
   full-band acceptance calculation or authorize fitting compensating EQ.
4. **Quiet decay has too little training weight.** The final silence occupies
   0.5/96 seconds and tail oversampling was zero. The refinement reserves 10%
   of crops for the last 0.8 seconds, retaining their true preceding history
   and captured targets. This is a sampling change, not invented silence or
   a change to the native DSP. The A2 field remains 6,347 samples (132.23 ms);
   recursive native tails can outlast it. Existing absolute quiet-residual
   limits remain unchanged; more sampling is not claimed to remove that
   representational limit.

The captured controls, all eight frozen native headers, native latency,
normalization reversal, A2 architecture, target engine and quality profile are
unchanged. No channel interpolation or EQ fitting is introduced. Preserving
these numerical references does not substitute for listening to the five
channel roles and macro responses.

## Implemented checks

- Every declared input/output file must match its SHA-256 before training.
  Reused input hashes across train/validation/test/audition are rejected.
- Resume identity v2 includes training settings; use explicit warm-start for
  older identities or changed settings instead of silently reusing optimizer
  moments under a different loss/crop/sampler configuration.
- A2 loss/gradient equivalence, output reparameterization, export-consistent
  centering, split leakage and resume drift tests run in the existing NAM CI
  failure-gate entry point. Local discovery: 46 test executions PASS (some
  suites are also imported by the CI entry point).
- Grouped/upstream output max error 2.98e-8; gradient max error 9.74e-9, both
  below 1e-6. Rebuilt native output is bit-identical to all five archived
  24-second validation references. Local offline tools reuse available JUCE
  objects and are not represented as a fresh full CMake/DAW build.

The paired refinement and fresh-test results are recorded separately after
completion. `release_approved=false` remains mandatory even if numerical gates
eventually pass. Real guitar DI, blind channel-role listening, TONE3000 GUI
load/save, and an actual DAW reopen/close/exit test remain manual blockers.
