# Náströnd A2 continued training on 16,320 s, 2026-10-27

This run warm-starts from the 7,680 s models
([continued-7680-20261027](continued-7680-20261027.md)). Training data grows
to 16,320 s: 34 captures of main `4ef21c16`, each with its own training seed,
appended in place with `append_captures.py`. It runs 40,000 steps, warming
the LR up from zero over 1,000 steps to a peak of 0.0002 and then halving it
every 12,000 steps. It selects on the full validation signal and uses profile
v2.

New in this run is `--keep-passed`. Every limit a channel's starting model
passed on validation becomes a hard gate: no step that fails one of them can be
selected, whatever its ESR or level. Kept limits: Fenrir level; Surtr and
Nidhoggr level and quiet; Fimbulvetr full ESR, active median, level and quiet;
Ragnarok quiet.

## Reading

- **Doubling the data again still helps, but less each time.** Mean test full
  ESR fell 3.6%, after falls of 14% and 7% for the two previous doublings.
  Data alone is close to its limit at this width.
- **No passed limit was lost.** Every channel kept all its kept limits on
  validation, and on test too. Fimbulvetr still passes full ESR on both
  splits and active median on validation.
- **Fimbulvetr keeps its start.** Its later steps reached a validation ESR of
  0.0065, but every one of them had a peak error of +1.02 to +1.17 dB, outside
  ±1 dB. The only step within the limit, 25,000 (+0.96 dB), scored 0.00679
  against the start's 0.00678.
- **Surtr's test ESR is flat** (0.01895 → 0.01898) although validation fell
  4.5%. Selection uses only validation; test is not used to choose models.
- **Ragnarok's peak error stays outside ±1 dB**, at +1.5 dB on test and +1.7
  to +2.0 dB on validation throughout the run. Training on ESR does not move it.
- With the LR warmup and a 0.0002 peak, the early dip after the warm start
  was smaller: mostly 5–20%, at worst 48% (Surtr, step 5,000), against more
  than 100% at the start of the 3,840 s run. The first new best came at step
  6,500, and most channels only passed their start after step 19,000.
- The active p95 (7–9× the limit; Fimbulvetr 2.6×) and worst limits remain far
  away.
