# Náströnd A2 v2, 50,000-step training, 2026-10-25

A2-Full, trained from scratch on the 480 s capture from main `4ef21c16`, under
quality profile `nastrond-tone3000-fidelity-v2`. **Numerical accuracy: FAIL on
all five.** No release approval. This is the best result so far: mean full ESR
0.032, against 0.068 after the 5,000-step refinement.

## Run

The run trained 50,000 steps at batch 2 with 4,096-frame crops. The LR started
at 0.004 with a 20,000-step half-life (0.0007 at the end). It used the legacy
recipe, 10% tail crops, centering and level-gated selection, on 4 CPU threads,
for about 4.6 hours of training.

At step 30,500 the run was paused and resumed with `e37b69f`. Until then,
excerpt selection had scored the raw network output, which still contains the
zero-input offset that export removes. Its scores swung between 0.3 and 1.3
while the exported response stayed near 0.04–0.05. On resume, the saved best
states were re-scored with the corrected metric.

## Reading

- **The training budget was the main limit.** Ten times more training halved
  the error of every channel. Returns are diminishing: from step 30,000 to step
  50,000 the mean full ESR fell only from 0.042 to 0.032. The final training loss (0.025) is now
  below the validation ESR (0.032), so the run has begun to generalize less well.
- **The active p95 and worst limits are the largest gap.** Every channel's p95
  is 6–18× its limit; the median is 2–5× its limit.
- **Ragnarok's peak overshoot is confined to attack transients.** At 40k steps,
  only 49 of 1.15 M samples exceeded the reference maximum, mostly on the first
  attack of loud plucks; at 0.92 s, for example, the model reached 4.31 where
  the reference is 3.49. Level-gated selection found no checkpoint within
  ±1 dB peak in the whole run.
- **Fenrir's quiet failure is one note release.** It sits at 23.51 s, just
  after the input stops, which is inside the model's memory and not a
  zero-history sample. Its peak error is −52.6 dBFS against a −60 dBFS limit.
- **Loud inputs still cost about twice the error** of quiet ones: pluck-window
  ESR around 0.07 against 0.013–0.036 at 40k steps.

## Note on earlier runs

The runs `train-v2`, `refine-v2` and `memory-diagnostic` selected checkpoints
with the raw-output excerpt score. Their reported numbers are correct for the
models they exported, but those may not be the best states the runs reached.
The memory comparison used the same method on both arms.
