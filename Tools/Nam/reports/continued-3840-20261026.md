## Reading

- **Doubling the data again helped.** Mean test full ESR is 14% lower and
  every channel improved. Fimbulvetr passes the full-ESR limit on both splits,
  and its validation active median of 0.0040 is within the limit; on test it
  is 0.0069.
- **Ragnarok's peak error is now +1.88 dB**, down from +2.31 dB but still
  outside ±1 dB.
- After the warm start, every channel got worse for about 10,000 steps (at
  step 500, Surtr went from 0.022 to 0.047) and only began improving once the
  LR fell to about 0.00035. Resetting the optimizer state at a warm start
  costs that much; a warmup of the LR from zero would probably avoid it.
- The active p95 (7–9× its limit, Fimbulvetr 3×) and worst limits remain far
  away, as the earlier diagnostics predicted.
